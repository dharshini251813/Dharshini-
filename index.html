Dear My Best Friend ❤️

Hey, my lovely gem! 💗

I honestly don't know where to start. How do I thank someone who has been such a big part of my life? How do I explain a friendship that means more to me than these few words can ever show?

We started as friends in 11th standard, but look at us now. We have come this far, survived so many moments, shared so much, and somehow continued to be a part of each other's lives. And that means more to me than you probably realise.

You are not just my best friend. You are my constant one. The person I associate with so many memories. The lovely gem I am genuinely grateful to have in my life. The one I want to thank, even when I cannot find the right words to do it.

Thank you for helping me. Thank you for standing beside me through the moments we have faced together. Thank you for the little things you did that might have seemed ordinary to you but meant something special to me.

Thank you for being there until now. Life is not always easy, and friendships are not always perfect. We grow, change, get busy, misunderstand each other, and face our own struggles. But I am grateful for the journey we have shared and for the memories that belong to us.

You have seen different sides of me. You have been part of a chapter of my life that I will always remember. When I think about my 11th-standard days, our friendship will always be one of the things that makes that chapter special.

I may not say it every day, but I appreciate you. I appreciate your presence, your help, your friendship, and the fact that our paths crossed. I don't want to treat a bond like ours as something ordinary just because time keeps moving.

And please remember this: you don't have to be perfect to be important to me. You don't have to do something extraordinary every day to deserve my gratitude. The fact that you are my friend and have been part of my journey is already something I value.

You are my best friend, my constant one, my lovely gem, my 11th-standard favourite, and my one and only. Thank you for everything, even the things I cannot explain in words. ❤️

Whatever the future brings, I hope we always remember the good we have shared. I wish you happiness, peace, success, and all the beautiful things you deserve.

Forever grateful for our friendship,
Dharshini 💗
